Weed Detection

AI-Based Analysis

Machine Learning Models: Analyze drone images to detect weed-infested areas automatically.

Precision Mapping: Generates detailed maps showing exact weed locations for targeted treatment.

Aerial Imaging

Multispectral and RGB Cameras: Drones capture high-resolution images to differentiate weeds from crops based on color, texture, and growth patterns.

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): Helps identify abnormal vegetation (like weeds) by measuring plant health.

Weed management

Eco-Friendly Practices

Reduced Soil Disturbance: Unlike tractors, drones do not compact soil or disturb crop rows.

Supports Organic Farming: Some drones can be used to apply organic weed control solutions or natural suppressants.

Precision Spraying

Targeted Herbicide Application: Drones spray only on the affected areas, reducing chemical use and saving costs.

Variable Rate Application: Adjusts herbicide quantity based on weed density.

Benefits

  • Cost Efficiency: Saves 30–50% on herbicide usage and labor.
  • Time Saving: Covers acres in minutes, ideal for large farms.
  • Higher Accuracy: Minimizes risk of damaging crops.
  • Environmentally Friendly: Reduces chemical runoff and overuse.

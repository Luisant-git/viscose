  • Disease and Pest Management

Surveillance & Monitoring

High-Resolution Imaging: Drones equipped with RGB, thermal, or multispectral cameras can detect early signs of disease or pest infestation.

Real-Time Analysis: Monitor crop health, spot affected areas quickly, and take immediate action.

Data Mapping: Generate NDVI or thermal maps to assess plant stress and infection levels.

Safe & Efficient

Reduces Human Exposure: Minimizes contact with harmful chemicals.

Time & Labor Saving: Drones cover large areas faster than manual methods.

Aerial Spraying

Targeted Application: Drones can spray pesticides, fungicides, and biological agents with precision.

Uniform Coverage: Ensures even spraying across crops, reducing under- or over-application.

Less Crop Damage: No need for human workers to walk through fields, minimizing plant damage.

Early Detection & Prevention

Spot Treatment: Only affected areas can be treated instead of the whole field, saving input costs and reducing chemical usage.

Forecasting Outbreaks: By analyzing patterns in drone data, farmers can predict potential outbreaks before they spread.

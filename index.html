About
& Viscose Crop Solution

Viscose Crop Solutions, in its commitment to providing consumers with direct access to farm-fresh, organic, and non-genetically modified produce, aligns its mission with a critical imperative within the global agricultural landscape—particularly in regions such as India. India, a nation deeply rooted in agrarian practices cultivated over millennia, possesses uniquely fertile terrain that supports an expansive agricultural sector. This sector, which directly employs over ninety million households, represents a cornerstone of the Indian economy and social structure. As such, any innovation—such as the technological integration championed by Viscose Crop Solutions—can have a profound impact on a significant segment of the population. The implementation of sustainable and environmentally conscious farming methods, therefore, holds immense potential not only for enhancing the quality and accessibility of produce but also for uplifting the livelihoods of a substantial portion of the Indian populace and fortifying the nation's long-term food security..

Difference between Synthetic Fertilizer AND Organic Fertilizer

Synthetic Fertilizer

  • Pesticide
  • Insecticides
  • Fungicides & Disinfectants
  • Herbicides
  • Biological Pest control agents
  • Liquid NPK ( Nitrogen, phosphorus,potassium )
  • Micronutrients ( Zinc, Boron. Etc.,)
  • Plant Growth Regulators
  • Weed control Agents
  • Pollination Enhancers / Bio- Stimulants

Organic Fertilizer

  • Liquid Compost Tea
  • Fish Emulsion
  • Seaweed Extract
  • Humic and Fulvic Acids
  • Vermiwash
  • Cow urine-based formulations
  • Microbial bio-fertilizers in liquid form
